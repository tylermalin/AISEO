Executive summary

Treat AI search as SEO plus reputation plus verification

Make sure the AI crawlers can reach you. Make your most exposed pages the clearest, best-sourced statement of their facts. Get referenced by credible third parties. Then measure how often you show up across many prompts, because a single "AI rank" is mostly noise.

Risks first

  • Most tactics on the market have no evidence behind them. Server logs show 97% of llms.txt files are never requested, and the one controlled test of adding schema found no citation lift.
  • Manipulation is already happening and is being treated as an attack. Microsoft found 31 companies hiding prompt injections in "Summarize with AI" buttons.
  • Ask an engine for brand recommendations twice and you get the same list less than once in 100 tries.
  • Where an AI Overview appears, organic clicks drop by roughly 40%.

Where it pays

  • Sourced claims can lift a lower-ranked page past the leader. In the founding GEO experiment, adding citations raised the fifth-ranked page's visibility 115% while the top page lost 30%.
  • Branded web mentions correlate with AI Overview visibility about three times as strongly as backlinks.
  • A brand cited inside an AI Overview gets 120% more organic clicks per impression than one that is left out.
  • Google's own guide says unique, first-hand content is the factor most likely to drive presence over time.
01 · The shift

The answer moved above the link

Agarwal (Indian School of Business) and Sen (Carnegie Mellon) used a Chrome extension to randomly hide AI Overviews for 1,065 US desktop users between January 7 and February 10, 2026. When an Overview showed, outbound organic clicks fell 39.8% and zero-click searches rose 34.5%. Sponsored clicks held flat.

The clicks that remained were not better clicks. Bounce rate, back-button returns and time on page matched across groups, which cuts against Google's claim that Overview clicks are higher quality. More than 95% of participants never noticed their results had changed.

02 · Mechanism

How answer engines choose sources

Answer engines retrieve passages, write one answer and attach the sources that support it. Ranking is now one input to that process. Google's May 2026 guide says its AI features run on its core ranking systems and names two techniques: retrieval-augmented generation and query fan-out. Click each stage.

03 · Content

What the GEO experiment actually tested

The 2023 paper by Aggarwal, Murahari and colleagues at Princeton, Georgia Tech, the Allen Institute and IIT Delhi named the field. They built a 10,000-query benchmark, fed the top five Google results to a GPT-3.5 answer engine, rewrote one source at a time with nine methods, and measured how much of the answer each source earned.

Exhibit 5 · GEO-bench, position-adjusted word count

Visibility change by rewrite method

Read with care

  • It is a 2023 lab result on GPT-3.5 over five fixed sources. Production engines in 2026 retrieve far more widely.
  • The paper's rewriting prompts allowed invented statistics and quotes, and the lab engine could not tell. Journalists, regulators and later models can. Use real data or none.
  • Effects vary by domain. Statistics helped most on law, government and opinion queries; quotations on people, society and history.

A citable passage

  • States one claim plainly near the top of its section, in a sentence that still makes sense when lifted out.
  • Carries a number, a date or a named source the model can attach to the claim.
  • Says something the model cannot produce on its own: first-party data, a measured result, a real decision and what followed.
  • Sits on a page that other credible sites also reference.
04 · Evidence ledger

Every common tactic, graded

Dots show evidence strength, from a single vendor correlation (one) to a controlled test or a platform statement plus independent data (five). Filter by verdict.

Answer engines can also be gamed in ways classic search could not. They read retrieved text as context, and models follow instructions found in text. Researchers at USC and Arizona State showed hidden, fluent text in a product description can push that product up an LLM's ranking. Microsoft's Defender team found more than 50 hidden prompt injections from 31 real companies across 14 industries. Treat anything you would not want a journalist to find in your page source as off limits.

06 · Measurement · Tool

    
Track presence across many runs

    
SparkToro and Gumshoe.ai ran the same recommendation prompts again and again through ChatGPT, Claude and Google's AI. The chance of getting an identical brand list twice was under 1 in 100. Position is noise. Frequency of appearance across a fixed prompt set is the signal worth tracking, the way a pollster tracks share rather than one interview.

    
Google's Search Console added a Generative AI report on June 3, 2026. It shows impressions in AI Overviews, AI Mode and Discover by page, country and device. It does not show clicks or the queries behind them.

08 · Failure modes

    
How AI search programs go wrong

    
The most expensive mistake is spending a year optimizing against a moving, partly unmeasurable target and counting the activity as progress.

  

  

    
Failure modeWhat it looks likeMitigation

    
      
Buying the checklistllms.txt, AI schema, chunked pages and a rank tracker sold as a packageHold each tactic to the evidence ledger. Demand a control group.

      
Fabricated evidenceStatistics and quotes added to look citable, without real sourcingEvery number traces to a source you can produce on request.

      
Scaled fan-out pagesHundreds of near-duplicate pages, one per sub-questionGoogle classes this as scaled content abuse. Build fewer, deeper pages.

      
Prompt injectionHidden instructions in page text, alt tags or "Summarize with AI" linksBan it in writing for staff and agencies.

      
Accidental invisibilityA CDN default or blanket robots.txt rule blocks search botsMonthly log check with separate training and search policies.

      
Vanity measurementOne "AI rank" or a screenshot of a flattering answerReport presence frequency across a fixed prompt set, plus conversions.

      
Over-rotationDropping classic SEO for AI-only workGoogle's AI features run on its core ranking systems.

      
Platform dependencyA model that needs clicks from answers that send fewer of themBuild owned channels: email, community, direct relationships.

      
MisrepresentationAn engine states your price or policy wrongMonthly review. Fix the source pages and listings it draws from.

    
  

  
Some risk sits above any one site. Publishers and platforms are renegotiating the terms of crawling through Cloudflare's pay-per-crawl model, EU and UK antitrust complaints, and suits like Penske Media's against Google. The rules on who gets paid, cited and allowed to opt out will likely settle over the next few years, so plan for them to change.







  
09 · Thesis

  
An engine that answers in its own voice carries the risk of being wrong, so it favors sources it can safely repeat.

  

    
For twenty-five years search rewarded the page that best matched a query and collected the most links. The engine only pointed, and the user judged. Answer engines took on the judging. One academic measurement already finds about one claim in nine unsupported by the pages an Overview cites, and every operator has reason to close that gap.

    
That explains how the evidence lines up. Statistics and named sources lift visibility because they can be checked. Third-party mentions predict inclusion because they corroborate. First-hand content wins because the model does not already have it. Prompt injection is being catalogued as an attack because it corrupts the checking.

    
The missing layer in AI search is provenance: a reliable way for a machine to know where a claim came from, who stands behind it and whether it held up. Organizations that publish measured, sourced and corroborated facts are building that layer for their own category. At scale, the incentives now reward accuracy and shown work, which good institutions valued long before search existed.
